# Open Tickets Brand Guide

## Brand idea

Open Tickets is a premium, digital-first event-access brand. The identity combines a notched ticket silhouette, an OT monogram, Midnight Navy and Solid Gold.

## Primary logo assets

- Light surfaces: `public/assets/logo/opentickets-logo-for-light.png`
- Dark surfaces: `public/assets/logo/opentickets-logo-for-dark.png`
- App/favicon mark: `public/icon.svg`
- High-resolution light master: `open-tickets-logo-brand-light.png`
- High-resolution dark master: `open-tickets-logo-brand-dark.png`

The primary identity uses the approved horizontal ticket outline, stacked OPEN TICKETS wordmark and gold perforation.

## Core colors

- Midnight Navy: `#041E2A`
- Near Black Navy: `#071319`
- Deep Teal: `#075064`
- Event Gold: `#F2C318`
- Solid Gold: `#D4AF37`
- Warm White: `#F6F4ED`
- Steel Grey: `#9AA7AB`

## Logo usage

- Use the light logo on Warm White, white or very pale backgrounds.
- Use the dark logo on Midnight Navy, Near Black Navy, photography overlays or other dark surfaces.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the height of the OT icon.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, add gradients or place the logo over visually noisy imagery without a dark overlay.
- At small sizes, use `public/icon.svg` rather than the full wordmark.

## Interface usage

- Primary CTA: Solid Event Gold with Near Black Navy text.
- CTA hover/focus: Solid Black with Event Gold text.
- Light-mode header: Warm White with Navy and Gold identity.
- Light-mode footer: Midnight Navy.
- Dark-mode surfaces: Near Black Navy and Deep Teal.
